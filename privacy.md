# Privacy Policy for Živý Obraz

Effective date: May 25, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Živý Obraz Android app handles user and device data.

## Data We Collect or Process

The app is used to display and manage data from the Živý Obraz service. To provide its functionality, the app may process:

- Živý Obraz export keys and optional import keys entered by the user
- e-paper device identifiers and device metadata returned by the Živý Obraz API
- sensor and status data such as temperature, humidity, pressure, battery level, firmware, last contact time, and display status
- optional phone battery status if the user enables phone battery push
- local diagnostic logs used for troubleshooting

The app does not collect location, contacts, calendar data, photos, videos, audio recordings, messages, browsing history, payment information, or health data.

## How We Use Data

The app uses the data only to provide app functionality, including:

- loading and displaying e-paper devices
- refreshing device status in the background
- showing home screen widgets
- optionally sending phone battery status to the Živý Obraz service
- helping troubleshoot app behavior through diagnostic logs

## Data Sharing

The app does not sell user data.

The app communicates with the Živý Obraz service using HTTPS endpoints operated for the app functionality:

- https://out.zivyobraz.eu/
- https://in.zivyobraz.eu/

Data is not shared with advertising networks or analytics providers.

## Data Storage

The app stores configuration and cached device data locally on the user's device. This may include export/import keys, device lists, aliases, visibility settings, refresh history, and diagnostic logs.

Users can remove stored account data from within the app by removing the configured account. Users can also uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from the device.

## Security

Data sent between the app and the Živý Obraz service is transmitted over HTTPS.

## Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
